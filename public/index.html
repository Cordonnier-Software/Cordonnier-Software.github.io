The name

Four generations of shoemakers

Cordonnier (kor-don-YAY) is French for shoemaker — the trade of four generations of the LeFort family, from Joseph Célestin Lefort, cordonnier in Montréal, through Gustave in Worcester's shoe shops, August, and Raymond Jr. at Ray's Shoe Repair.

A shoemaker's work is measured, fitted, and made to last — and repaired rather than replaced. That's the standard this software is built to.

How I build

Three habits

Self-hosted where it can be

Your data lives on your hardware, under your control — not rented back to you from someone else's cloud.

Evidence over assumption

Conclusions trace back to sources. If a record can't be shown, it isn't claimed.

Built for a real problem

No software for its own sake. Each project earns its place by being used, daily, by the person who built it.
